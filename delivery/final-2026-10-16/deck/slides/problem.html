The problem · relocation providers

Relocation providers stop at week six. The hard months come after.

When a company sends an employee abroad, it pays a relocation provider to move the family: flat, visa paperwork, registration, schools.

Weeks 0 to 6

The provider is on contract and on call.

Months 3 to 12

Kita and school, health insurance, the partner’s work rights, a job search, loneliness. Nobody is on contract any more.

The provider is judged on whether the assignment worked, but its contract ends around week six. The administrative and personal events that decide the outcome sit in months three to twelve. Beyond Arrival is the provider’s own branded companion for that stretch, so it is a revenue line for them, not a cost.
