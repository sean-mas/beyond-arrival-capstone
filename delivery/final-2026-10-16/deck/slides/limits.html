Limits and next steps

What I would build next, in this order

1 · Who is speaking about whom

The family profile never reaches the model yet. 20 test cases are written: same words, different family, different right answer.

2 · Memory between messages

A guiding question only helps if the next message knows it was asked.

3 · A fresh, unfitted test set

New posts labelled against the written handoff definition, to measure recall and the over-referral honestly.

4 · A judge I can trust

More “no” examples in my labels, not a bigger judge: with 29 of 30 grounded, Cohen’s κ stays at chance.

Each of these came out of the measurements, not a wish list. Personalisation is first because labelling showed the most visible failures: advice about school to a couple without children, or answering the partner when the message was about the daughter.
