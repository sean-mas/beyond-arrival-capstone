Runs on this laptop: local database and search, hosted models. The IBAN is the standard published example number.

Open localhost:8000/chat?demo=fresh: welcome, “Make it yours”, the five onboarding questions (about 30 seconds), then the journey for ten seconds, then Chat. The demo buttons offer each message in turn. At the IBAN step switch to Inspector once to show the redacted message and the cards. Tested this week: answers in about 5 seconds, handover and refusal in under 1 second. Backup: the recorded video.
