## 1 · The problem

### The precise version

There is one window, roughly **month three to month twelve after arrival**, that nobody
owns: not contractually, not technically, not emotionally. Support is dense before it and
absent inside it, while difficulty runs the other way.

Three different people feel that window, and they feel three different problems. Blurring
them is what made the earlier framings vague.

| Who | Their problem | Do they have a budget line? |
|---|---|---|
| **The accompanying partner** | Gave up work to follow. No colleagues, no structure, often no language. The pain peaks after the logistics are solved, when everyone has stopped helping and the employee is at work all day. | **No.** Nobody sells to them because the assumption is the employer handled it. |
| **The relocation provider** | Judged by the corporate client on whether the assignment worked, while their contract ended around week six. Held responsible for an outcome they have no contract to influence, and cannot sell aftercare because aftercare means a human on retainer, which destroys the margin. | **Yes**, and it is a revenue line, not a cost line. |
| **The employer** | Pays for the move, notices only when the assignment fails or the hire leaves. | **Yes**, but reached by Benivo, Localyze and Topia, not by a solo founder (rt1). |

### The one-sentence problem

> A relocating family's support ends at week six, the hard part starts at month four, and
> the only party with both a budget and a reason to care about the gap is the relocation
> provider whose contract already closed.

### What is NOT yet evidenced, and matters

The economic weight of the problem is currently **uncited**. The widely repeated "nearly
40 percent of repatriates leave within a year" figure was checked and is not on the page
it is attributed to (decision-log, 29.08.2026, killed claim). So the repo has:

- verified evidence that budgets exist and are growing (niche:ev_13, 61 percent increasing
  relocation budgets)
- verified evidence that AI is being adopted in mobility (niche:ev_12, n=302, 79 percent
  using AI, ~6 percent embedded)
- **no verified figure for what a failed assignment costs, or how often one fails**

That is the number the whole problem statement leans on rhetorically. Treat it as a TODO,
not as a known fact, and never put it in a pitch until it is cited. Its absence is also a
reason to keep the Track A email arguing from the provider's own revenue rather than from
assignment-failure statistics, which is how it is currently written.

### Scope decided 11.09.2026: tangible, not culture-only

The window is defined by events, not by moods. Naming them is what makes the problem
statement checkable by a provider against their own files rather than taken on trust.

| Inside the window, outside the provider's contract | Why it lands there |
|---|---|
| Residence-permit renewal | The permit was issued at arrival and runs down inside the first year |
| The move out of temporary housing | The first flat is usually serviced or interim. The real Mietvertrag, Schufa and Kaution come later |
| The first Nebenkostenabrechnung | Arrives roughly twelve months after move-in and is routinely a shock |
| Kindergeld, Krankenkasse, Steuerklasse | Nobody handles these in week two and nobody chases them afterwards |
| The first full school year | Runs its length regardless of when the file closed |

**The guardrail, and it is not negotiable.** Advice on someone's individual case is a
Rechtsdienstleistung under the German RDG and is reserved for licensed providers. So the
product names deadlines, lists what the authority itself publishes, explains vocabulary and
says what to ask. It does not assess a case, decide anything, complete forms on anyone's
behalf or represent anyone. The same line applies to tenancy and tax. Crossing it triggers a
handoff back to the provider, which is a feature: that is where their own consultant or
lawyer becomes billable, and it is the mechanism behind the positioning that this adds to
their service rather than replacing it.

## 2 · The audience

### Three people. One payer, one champion, one excluded.

**The payer · owner-operator of a 5 to 50 person German relocation provider**

The person who signs. Usually the founder, often the person answering the phone. Cares
about margin, differentiation against the provider down the road, and staying inside the
corporate account between moves. Does not care about intercultural theory.

Where they are online:

| Channel | What it gives | Status |
|---|---|---|
| EuRA member directory | German members with addresses (niche:ev_14) | enumerated |
| iamexpat.de relocation directory | named German providers | enumerated |
| expatservicedesk.de regional directories | highest-yield route to the remaining pool per `../../private/market-test/smoke-test.md` | partly worked |
| Europages | the 1,659 pool (ev_06) | source of record |
| LinkedIn | owner-operators by company, 5-50 headcount | not yet worked |
| Company Impressum pages | the email addresses the pipeline is missing | **34 of 35 still blank** |

**They are not in forums.** No amount of community listening reaches this person. Only the
twenty emails do.

**The champion and user · the accompanying partner**

Not the employee. The partner who followed. This is the person in pain, the person who
would use the product daily, and the person with no budget line. In Track A they are the
reason the provider's client renews. In Track B they are the buyer.

Where they are online:

| Channel | Size | Note |
|---|---|---|
| expats.de Facebook groups (15) | ~500k memberships, heavy overlap (niche:ev_19) | Expats in Germany 145k+, Berlin 85k+, Munich 60k+, Frankfurt 35k+ (highest corporate-assignee density) |
| r/expats | ~251k (niche:ev_19) | searchable history, hostile to promotion |
| r/germany, r/IWantOut, r/AmerExit | large | participate before posting |
| InterNations | 26 German cities, ~6k events/month | the offline route |
| The Trailing Spouse Co | small | smallest, warmest, most precisely on target |
| Toytown Germany | unknown | long-running English-language Germany forum with deep searchable history. **TODO: verify it is still active before counting it** |

**The excluded · the employee**

The person with the job has the most support and the least pain. They are not the audience.
Say this out loud in interviews, because every incumbent is built around them: Carla is
priced per employee seat (rt7).

**The abandoned · corporate HR and global mobility**

Explicitly not the buyer. Benivo, Localyze and Topia all sell there on contact-sales
pricing (rt1). That plan is dead by decision, not by oversight.

## 3 · What you can validate from forums right now, for free

This is the highest-value thing available today, and it is not a substitute for Track A.

### What forum listening CAN settle

1. **Whether "month four" is real or your own story.** The entire product premise rests on
   difficulty peaking after support ends. If families instead talk overwhelmingly about
   Anmeldung, visas and flat-hunting, the premise moves.
2. **Whether the partner, not the employee, is the person in trouble.**
3. **The exact vocabulary.** `../../private/market-test/smoke-test-b2c.md` already says the words people use go
   straight into the landing page. Passive search gets you those words without posting.
4. **What they do instead today.** The real alternative: asking strangers in a Facebook
   group, paying a coach, therapy, or going home.
5. **Whether the relocation provider has any emotional salience at all** in how families
   describe the move.

### What it CANNOT settle

- **a2, the kill assumption.** No family in a forum knows whether a provider would resell
  third-party software. Only the twenty emails settle that.
- **Price.** Stated willingness to pay in a forum is worthless.

So: forum work is **problem validation**, not business validation. Do it because it makes
the interviews sharper and the landing page truer, not because it replaces Track A.

### Why it is worth real effort: it fixes your weakest evidence

Demand currently scores 3 of 3 on niche:ev_12 and niche:ev_13, which measure AI appetite
among mobility professionals. rt6 already concedes those say nothing about families wanting
this. Forum posts are primary sources with URLs and quotable excerpts, which means they fit
`evidence.schema.json` directly. Twenty of them would replace a borrowed demand signal with
a cited one.

### The searches to run

Google, because in-platform search is worse than site-restricted Google:

```
site:reddit.com/r/expats "trailing spouse" Germany
site:reddit.com/r/expats Germany "gave up my career"
site:reddit.com/r/expats "six months in" lonely
site:reddit.com/r/germany "my wife" OR "my husband" "no friends"
site:reddit.com/r/germany "moved here for my husband's job"
site:reddit.com/r/germany "relocation package" ended OR stopped
site:reddit.com/r/AmerExit Germany partner regret
"trailing spouse" Germany depressed forum
"expat partner" Germany "no job" "no friends"
```

Inside the Facebook groups, search each group for: `trailing spouse`, `partner no job`,
`lonely`, `month four`, `six months`, `regret moving`, `school problems`, `going back home`.

### Pre-register the reading, before you look

Do this now, in this file, so it cannot be rationalised afterwards. Same discipline as G4.

> **Target: 20 posts collected via the searches above, each recorded with URL and excerpt.**
>
> - At least **12 of 20** place the hardest point **after week six**, not during arrival.
> - At least **8 of 20** describe the **accompanying partner** as the person in trouble,
>   rather than the employee.
> - Record how many mention a relocation provider or agency at all. No threshold, because
>   there is no prior. It is a number worth having before the provider calls.
>
> If fewer than 12 place the pain after week six, the month-four thesis is your own story
> and the problem statement needs rewriting before any outreach goes out.

**Result: not yet run.**

### Rules for looking without burning the channel

Passive search burns nothing and needs no permission. Do all of it before posting anything.
The posting rules in `../../private/market-test/smoke-test-b2c.md` (read pinned rules, message the admin, ask rather
than announce, one group per week) apply the moment you stop reading and start writing.
