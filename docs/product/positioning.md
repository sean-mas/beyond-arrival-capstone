# Positioning

What the product claims, who it claims it to, and where each claim sits. Assembled
21.09.2026 from `04-side-project/ideas/relocation-family-companion/summary.md` and the live
partners page at `04-side-project/ideas/relocation-family-companion/landing/partners/index.html`.

The page and the messages are **frozen**. The redesign window closed 15.09.2026 when Wave 1
went out, and it was pre-registered on 01.09 so a result cannot be explained away afterwards
by pointing at the wording. Nothing here changes before 15.10.

## The buyer

The **owner-operator of a 5 to 50 person German relocation provider**, frequently the person
answering the phone. Titles are Inhaberin, Geschaeftsfuehrer, or nothing. They care about
margin, differentiation against the firm down the road, and staying inside the corporate
account between placements. They do not care about intercultural theory.

Deliberately **not** corporate HR, where Benivo, Localyze and Topia sell on contact-sales
pricing. Deliberately **not** the family: that track was retired unstarted on 09.09.2026.

## The five messages

| # | Message | Where it sits on the page |

| 1 | Your contract ends before the outcome you are measured on. | hero, timeline |
| 2 | You already know aftercare would close it. The reason you do not sell it is headcount, and software is what removes the headcount. | "Why I think you might want it", first bullet |
| 3 | It carries your brand, and we never go near your client. | claim line, "What this is not" |
| 4 | It keeps you in the corporate account between moves. | "Why I think you might want it", second bullet, **marked on the page as unverified reasoning** |
| 5 | Nothing exists yet, and a handful of providers get to shape what it has to do. | "Where this honestly stands" |

Message 4 is the strongest commercial argument available and the one with the least evidence
behind it. It is a call question, not a claim: the repo holds nothing on how German providers
are actually re-tendered.

## The three strands, as the page names them

Orientation, life setup, cultural companion. Named on the page since 11.09.2026 alongside a
short GDPR and AI Act section. The full reading is in `compliance.md`.

## What the page does not say, on purpose

- **No assignment-failure rate.** The widely repeated "nearly 40 percent of repatriates leave
  within a year" figure was traced and is not on the page it is attributed to. Killed
  29.08.2026. The repo holds **no** verified figure for what a failed assignment costs or how
  often one fails, and the page says that absence out loud rather than filling it.
- **No claim to monitor, prevent or treat anything.** Gate G5, in
  `04-side-project/gates.md`. See `../constraints.md`.
- **No experience claim.** "Ich beschaeftige mich seit Jahren mit interkultureller
  Vorbereitung" sat in every outreach draft and was removed before sending on 15.09. The
  certification and degree are real; the implied track record was not.

## The build status the page commits to

The page states plainly that the first version is a capstone due 16.10, usable end of
October, and free for anyone who gives input. That is a dated promise made to eight named
providers. It is the reason the 02.10 deploy milestone in `../timeline.md` is not movable.
