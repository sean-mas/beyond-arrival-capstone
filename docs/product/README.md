## Two contradictions this project carries on purpose

Both were accepted knowingly on 15.09.2026. A coach or an examiner will find them, so they
are written down here rather than discovered in the Q&A.

**1. The T2 decision memo says do not build this yet.** `04-side-project/ideas/relocation-family-companion/decision-memo.md`
concludes: proceed to the market test, do not write code, feasibility is not the risk here,
distribution is, and "it is not yet worth a month of building". A month of building is
exactly what starts today. The reason is that the month is paid for by the bootcamp and the
thesis rather than by the business, so the build cost is sunk regardless and the market test
runs in parallel rather than before. That is a real answer, and it does not make the memo
wrong.

**2. The premise is not supported by the adjustment literature.** The pitch says the hard
part is months three to twelve. The U-curve model behind that, honeymoon then crisis then
recovery, does not survive testing: Ward et al. (1998) found distress **highest at entry**
and declining; Bhaskar-Shrinivas et al. (2005) tested it across 66 studies and 8,474
expatriates and did not support it; Church (1982) had already called the support weak. Full
citations with DOIs in `../../content/README.md`.

The corpus was written around that finding and **no card promises a dip at month six**. The
marketing copy was not. What survives the literature is the contractual argument, not the
psychological one: the provider's contract closes around week six and the administrative
events that decide the assignment (permit renewal, the move out of temporary housing,
Nebenkostenabrechnung, Kindergeld, Krankenkasse, tax class) fall after it. That version is
checkable by a provider against their own files. Lead with it.

## What is still unevidenced, and must stay out of every deck

- **What a failed assignment costs, and how often one fails.** No verified figure exists
  here. Harzing traced the folklore versions through the citation network and found
  misquotation rather than data. One statistic of exactly this shape was already killed in
  this repo on 29.08.2026.
- **Whether a provider will resell a third-party companion** (assumption a2, the kill
  assumption). Untested. Only the 22 emails settle it.
- **Price for this exact job.** Unobserved. Only a call settles it.
