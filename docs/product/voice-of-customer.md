## The one reply, and why it is the most useful thing here

A North Rhine-Westphalia provider, 16.09.2026. Classed **problem confirmed, budget path
refuted. Not a commitment.**

*The company and the person are named only in `../../private/market-test/replies/`. This
file is published, and one owner-operator answering a cold email did not agree to appear in
a public repository.*

| What they said | What it does |
|---|---|
| The post-arrival window is recognisable from their own practice | **Confirms the problem.** First operator confirmation, from someone selling into it |
| Their existing e-coaching is "nur sehr selten" booked by corporate clients | **Refutes the revenue path.** They already built the thing this test assumes providers want |
| Not for lack of need, but because corporate budget concentrates on immigration, housing and Anmeldung | **Names the mechanism.** The constraint is the budget line, not the price and not the need |
| Partner offerings are "positiv bewertet, aber nicht haeufig beauftragt" | Praised, not purchased |

This cuts deeper than a refusal. "We would build it ourselves" kills the channel and leaves
the product. "Nobody needs it" kills the product and leaves the channel. This kills neither
and undercuts both: everyone agrees it is needed, nobody pays, and the reason is structural.

It created assumption **a6**, aftercare is a revenue line rather than a cost line, which
`problem-and-audience.md` asserts and which is now contested by the only evidence that
exists on it.

It also cost something concrete: **they could not open the site.** A `netlify.app` subdomain
did not resolve for them.

## What it does not settle

One provider, one corridor, one price point, and an e-coaching product delivered by a human
coach, which is exactly the cost structure this product claims to remove. One reply is not a
result, and 14 of the 22 have not been contacted at all.

## Say this in the deck before a coach says it for you

- The eval set was written by the person who built the system. The 40 adversarial cases were
  written first to reduce that, and it is still a limitation.
- Nobody has agreed to resell this. The distribution assumption, a2, is the one that would
  kill it and it remains untested.
- The chat window is a demo surface. **The scheduler is the mechanism.** The family in month
  four does not open apps.
