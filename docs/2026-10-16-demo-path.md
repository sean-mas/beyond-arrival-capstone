# Demo path, final presentation 16.10.2026

Four minutes, **from the laptop**, not Render: the hosted embedder has no
credits left (402), and the laptop runs the same search with the local bge-m3.
Every step below was run on 14.10.2026 against the local app; answers are
written by a model, so the wording changes from run to run.

## Before you start (10 minutes before)

Use `make demo`, not `make run`: `make run` takes the crisis check from
`.env`, which is the local llama3.1 8B, while the deck, the measurements and
every demo message tested on 15.10 use qwen3.8 27B on Groq.

```bash
open -a Docker                 # wait until the whale icon is steady
ollama serve                   # in its own terminal, if Ollama is not already running
make db
make demo                      # http://localhost:8000/chat?demo=fresh
```

Then send one message, so the first answer in front of the room is not the
slow cold start. Have the backup video open in a second window.

## The three demo links

| Link | What it does |
|---|---|
| `localhost:8000/chat?demo=fresh` | Clears the family on this browser and starts at the welcome screen, so the onboarding can be shown. Demo buttons on |
| `localhost:8000/chat?demo=1` | A ready-made family (Sean, Ana, Leo), straight to the journey. Demo buttons on |
| `localhost:8000/chat?demo=0` | Demo buttons off |

In demo mode the chat offers the next scripted message as a button, with the
reason it is in the demo above it, so nothing has to be typed.

## The path

| # | Show | Type or tap | What happens (14.10) | Say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The journey | Open `/chat?demo=1`, ten seconds on the journey | A family, 12 days before the move | "The provider's own companion, after their contract ends." |
| 2 | An answer | Chat, tap **How do I register our address?** | Answer from the cards plus one next step, ~5 s | "Only from the provider's material, and it ends in one step." |
| 3 | The feeling | *My husband loves his new job here and I just feel lost. Is that normal?* | Common for accompanying partners, from the research cards, one gentle step; answered, not handed over (3 of 3 runs, 15.10), ~4 s | "The hard months are about feelings, not paperwork." |
| 4 | Personal data | *Our Kita wants the fees by direct debit. My IBAN is DE89 3704 0044 0532 0130 00, is that how it normally works?* | Fixed notice first: removed, do not share here, the Kita asks on its own form | "Removed in code before any model reads it." Switch to **Inspector** once: the message as the model saw it |
| 5 | A person | *My wife hasn't left the flat for a week and barely talks to me.* | Handover in under 1 s, no model wrote the reply | "Decided by the crisis check, the wording is fixed." |
| 6 | Where advice stops | *Will my residence permit be approved if I change jobs?* | Refusal plus handover in under 1 s | "Individual legal advice is reserved to licensed people in Germany." |

The IBAN is the standard published example number, not a real account.

## Do not use in the demo

These go to a person, which is safe but not what the scene is meant to show.
It is the price of the handoff recall of 0.92, and it is on the safety slide:

- "It's month four and I still don't really have friends here…"
- "My days here have no shape…"
- "We've been here six months. Is it normal that it still doesn't feel like home?"

## If something goes wrong

| What | Do |
|---|---|
| "the backend is not ready" | Docker or the database is down: `make db`, wait 10 s, retry |
| An answer hangs past 20 s | Ollama Cloud is slow: talk over it, or switch to the video |
| Every message goes to a person | Groq is over its daily limit (`make budget`): switch to the video |
| A passcode prompt | It comes from `DEMO_PASSCODE` in `.env`; enter it once per tab |
