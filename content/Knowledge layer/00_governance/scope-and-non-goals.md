# Scope and Non-Goals

## Goal
To provide a modular, source-backed knowledge specification on intercultural competence. This serves as a trainer-friendly playbook that is chunked and tagged so agents and RAG pipelines can reliably retrieve principles, models, and concrete activities.

## Scope
- **Core Definitions**: Establishing what intercultural competence (IC) and intercultural communicative competence (ICC) mean in 2026.
- **Competency Domains**: Cognitive, Affective, Behavioral, and Communicative.
- **Activity Library**: A set of structured, generalizable experiential activities based on canonical principles (e.g., Berardo & Deardorff) but updated for modern contexts.
- **Assessment Strategies**: Multi-dimensional, longitudinal rubrics focusing on reflection and behavior change.
- **Ethics & Power**: Emphasizing DEI, intersectionality, and UNESCO's rights-based frameworks.
- **Digital Modalities**: Best practices for Virtual Exchange, COIL, and AI-mediated scenarios.

## Non-Goals
- **Static Country Scoring**: We explicitly exclude static Hofstede-style country comparison tables that reduce cultures to rigid numbers.
- **Copyright Infringement**: This knowledge base synthesizes principles and concepts but does not distribute copyrighted texts or book chapters verbatim.
- **Simplistic Assessments**: Pre/post quizzes focused solely on "facts" about other cultures are out of scope. We assess reflection and behavioral adaptation.
