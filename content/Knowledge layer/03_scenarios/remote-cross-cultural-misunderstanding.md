# Scenario: Remote Cross-Cultural Misunderstanding

## Context
A global NGO is hosting a virtual strategic planning session via Zoom. The facilitator, based in London, expects rapid "popcorn-style" brainstorming. Participants from Japan and Indigenous communities in Canada remain largely silent, occasionally using the chat function. The facilitator assumes they are disengaged or lack ideas, and pushes them by cold-calling. This results in brief, uncomfortable answers.

## Conflict Points
1. **Turn-taking and Silence:** Viewing silence as empty space to be filled vs. silence as respect, processing time, or giving space to elders/leaders.
2. **Digital Modality Bias:** "Popcorn style" favors native speakers with low-context, high-interruption communication norms.

## RAG/Agent Application
*Equity/Power Lens Application:*
- Does the "norm" of the meeting benefit the dominant group? 
- How can the AI agent recommend hybrid facilitation techniques (e.g., anonymous digital whiteboards, structured turn-taking, asynchronous prep) to create an inclusive environment?
