# Scenario: Feedback and Conflict

## Context
Maria, a senior engineer from Colombia, recently moved to the Netherlands to join a new engineering pod. During her first code review, her Dutch peer, Lars, leaves over 30 comments. Lars's comments are highly direct: "This logic is wrong," "Rewrite this completely," "Inefficient." Maria feels personally attacked and alienated, assuming her technical competence is being questioned. She withdraws from team discussions. Lars is confused, as he considers this standard, efficient technical feedback and thinks highly of Maria's overall architecture.

## Conflict Points
1. **Feedback Norms:** Task-focused, direct negative feedback vs. relationship-focused, softened feedback.
2. **Attribution Error:** Maria attributes Lars's feedback to personal animosity or lack of respect; Lars attributes Maria's withdrawal to lack of engagement.

## RAG/Agent Application
*Intervention focus:*
- Empathy and Perspective-Taking (Affective Domain).
- Suggesting a structured reflection where Maria and Lars map their cultural feedback expectations.
- How can the agent guide Lars to adapt his delivery (Communicative Domain) without compromising technical rigor?
