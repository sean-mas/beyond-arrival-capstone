# Scenario: Global Team Collaboration

## Context
A multinational software development team is split between San Francisco, Berlin, and Bangalore. The project is behind schedule. The San Francisco product manager, Alex, sends frequent, urgent Slack messages at all hours and demands public status updates in the daily standup. The Bangalore team members often say "we are trying our best" but do not raise blockers publicly. The Berlin lead, Klaus, sends a blunt email to Alex stating the process is chaotic and inefficient. 

## Conflict Points
1. **Communication Styles:** Direct vs. Indirect (Klaus vs. Bangalore team).
2. **Power Dynamics:** HQ (San Francisco) dictating the urgency and tool usage without adjusting to local time zones.
3. **Face-Saving/Psychological Safety:** Public reporting of failure (standups) may not be culturally safe for all team members.

## RAG/Agent Application
*Prompt the user to analyze this scenario using the OSEE framework:*
- **Observe:** What actions actually occurred? (Emails sent, Slack messages sent, specific phrases used).
- **Interpret:** What might "we are trying our best" mean in a high-context culture vs a low-context culture?
- **Evaluate:** How should Alex adapt his behavioral and communicative approach to repair trust?
