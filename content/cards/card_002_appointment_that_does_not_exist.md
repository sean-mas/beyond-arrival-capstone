The two week registration deadline is federal. Appointment availability is local. In
several large cities those two things do not fit together, and the calendar shows
nothing for the next month or more.

This is a structural mismatch, not something you did wrong. People describe opening the
booking system and finding, as one put it, "only red marks on the calendar screen, for
the next 6 weeks or so". Another, trying to book a residence permit appointment, wrote
that there were "no online places for the next year".

## What people report actually working

Check the booking system early in the morning, when cancellations from the previous day
are released.

Look at offices in other districts. Most cities let you register at any Bürgeramt rather
than only the one nearest you, and availability varies a lot between them.

Write to the office by email and ask for a slot. For residence permit appointments this
is the documented route in some cities rather than a workaround.

Keep a record of every attempt with dates and screenshots. Some cities treat the date
you booked rather than the date you attend as the relevant one. That is administrative
practice in some places and not a legal rule, so do not rely on it, but you want the
evidence either way.

## When this stops being an inconvenience

If a residence permit is expiring and no appointment exists before it does, that is a
different problem and a more urgent one. The same is true if a job offer or a school
place depends on a document that cannot be obtained in the time available. Say so and
we will get a person involved rather than leaving you with booking tips.
