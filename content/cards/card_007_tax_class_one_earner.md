When you marry, both of you are placed in class IV automatically. Where one of you earns
nothing or much less, two other combinations are available and you may choose freely
between the three.

The common one is class III for the earner and class V for the other partner. That puts
the larger allowances against the salary, so less tax is withheld each month and take
home pay rises. A third option applies the same effect month by month using a factor.

## The part people are not told

None of this changes what you owe. The tax class only decides how much is withheld from
each payslip. The real figure is settled in your annual tax return, and a difference
either way comes back as a refund or a bill.

It is a cash flow choice, not a tax saving, and anyone telling you otherwise is selling
something.

## Where it does have real consequences

Payments calculated from your net salary. Parental allowance, unemployment benefit and
sick pay are all worked out that way, so the class you are in during the months before
one of those begins can matter considerably.

If a baby, a job change or a period of illness is foreseeable, the arithmetic is worth
doing properly rather than by default. This is the one situation where the choice is not
neutral.

## What to do

Changes are applied for at your local Finanzamt and can be made during the year.

Ask us how the mechanism works. Ask the Finanzamt or a Steuerberater what you should
choose, because that depends on numbers we do not have and should not guess at.
