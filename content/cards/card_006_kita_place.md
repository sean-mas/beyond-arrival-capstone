Two things are true at once here, and holding both is the whole picture.

The right is real. From the age of one until the third birthday a child has a legal
entitlement to early years support, in a Kita or with a registered childminder. From
three until school starts the entitlement continues. It is owed by the local youth
welfare office and it is enforceable.

The supply gap is also real and the government publishes it. At March 2025 about 37,8
percent of under threes were in some form of care, while just under half of parents of
under threes reported needing it. People describe waiting a year and being told a year
is optimistic.

What the entitlement does not promise is a place at the Kita you chose, a place near
home, or the hours you need.

## What to do

Find out how your municipality actually allocates places, because this is set locally
and not nationally. Some cities run one central portal. Others expect you to apply to
every Kita individually, and some expect a visit first.

Register as early as you possibly can, including before you arrive if the portal allows
it. With lead times of six to twelve months, registering early matters more than
choosing well.

Apply widely rather than to your three favourites.

## Worth knowing this year

There is now also an entitlement to all day care for primary school children, phasing in
one year group at a time from the 2026/2027 school year. If you have a child starting
school, that is new and directly relevant.

If a return to work is waiting on a place, say so. That changes what is worth doing next.
