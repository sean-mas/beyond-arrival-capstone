Almost everyone builds their first circle here out of other new arrivals. It happens
naturally, because you are all looking at the same time and nobody needs a two year run
up to become a Bekannte first. It is also the right thing to do, and anyone telling you
to hold out for local friends instead is costing you a year of having nobody.

The part worth knowing in advance is what happens next. International circles turn over.
Contracts end, assignments rotate, people go home. A common experience is that somewhere
around eighteen months to two years the circle you built empties out over a few months,
and you are rebuilding from close to zero, except now you are not new and nobody is
arriving alongside you. This is also roughly when people report homesickness arriving.
People report it arriving late, in year two rather than month two, and then staying.

## What people actually do

Run both tracks from early on. Keep the international friendships, they are real and
they are what gets most people through the first year. And put one slow, local,
scheduled thing in place alongside them, knowing it will not pay off for a year. The
people who found the second year easier are the ones who started that in the first.

Join something rooted in the place rather than in the situation. A Verein, a
Volkshochschule course, a choir, a neighbourhood initiative. Members are there because
they live there, not because they were posted there, so the group survives the rotation.

Ask leaving friends to introduce you before they go. Handovers happen and nobody thinks
to ask for one.

## If it stays

When homesickness is still there long after the circle has changed, it is worth taking
seriously rather than waiting out. Try to name what exactly is missed, a person, a place,
a way of living, because each points somewhere different. And say it to a person rather
than carrying it alone: that is what talking to someone at the provider is for, and it is
not an escalation. If it has turned into a question of whether to stay, raise it while it
is still a question.

## What does not work

Assuming the circle is permanent because it formed quickly, and starting the local track
only after it has emptied.
