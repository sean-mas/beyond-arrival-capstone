There are things this is good for and things it is not, and the line is worth stating
rather than discovering.

This can tell you how a process works, what a rule generally says, what other families
have found, and where to look next. That is information and preparation. It cannot tell
you what you should do in your own case. Advice on an individual legal, visa or tax
situation is reserved in Germany to people licensed to give it, which is a protection
for you rather than a limitation on us. It is also not a counselling service and does
not pretend to be one.

## How to reach a person

Ask, in any words, at any point. There is a handover path and using it is not an
escalation or an admission of anything, including when the only reason is that you would
rather talk to someone. Say so and it gets passed on.

## When it is worth asking

When a deadline is about to pass and the system will not let you meet it. When your
permit status feels uncertain. When a decision has gone against you and you do not know
what comes next. When you are being asked to decide something expensive and hard to
reverse. And when things at home are harder than the practical problems account for.

One research finding is relevant to the last one. What predicts a family leaving early
is not how well anyone has settled, it is how satisfied they are with the whole
arrangement. Which means saying it early, while it is still a question rather than a
decision, is worth more than waiting until you are sure.
